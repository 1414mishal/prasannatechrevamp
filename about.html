About Prasanna Technologies

Two Decades of Utility Sector Engineering

A technology company focused on mission-critical software for electricity boards, water utilities, and public revenue departments — with a track record built over 20+ years and 105 completed deployments.

Our Mission

Provide unparalleled consulting services that drive growth and efficiency

Prasanna Technologies was established to bring technology driven precision to the operational challenges of India's utility sector. Our focus has always been on sectors where software reliability has direct consequences on electricity distribution, water management, and public revenue administration.

Over two decades, we have evolved from a local software consultancy into a multi country technology partner, while keeping our core commitment: building software that operators actually rely on, day to day.

Founded with the singular goal of improving the efficiency of utility companies, that purpose has never wavered. At the centre of our portfolio sits Styra, a state of the art Project Monitoring and Workforce Tracking system built for conceptualising, designing, engineering, and managing digital products for high growth companies, and Energysync, a modular platform serving electrical utilities and urban local bodies. Together, these products reflect a practice driven by deep client partnership and measurable, sustained results.

Our Journey

Building Experience Over Time

Foundation

Established in India

Prasanna Technologies was founded with a focus on bringing software solutions to the operational challenges of Karnataka's electricity distribution sector.

Scale

BESCOM, MESCOM & KPTCL Deployments

Major workforce and revenue management deployments with Karnataka's largest electricity utilities, establishing our position as a trusted sector partner.

Expansion

Water Utilities & Reach Across Multiple Countries

Extended our platform to water utilities (MCC, UCMC) and grew our footprint to 4+ countries, with 450+ qualified staff serving clients across the region.

Today

105 Projects. ISO Certified. CMMI Level 3.

With ISO 9001, ISO 27001, and CMMI Level 3 certifications, and 105 completed projects across utilities and revenue management, we continue to grow and deepen our sector expertise.
