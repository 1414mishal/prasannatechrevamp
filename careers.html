Hiring Process

How We Hire

A straightforward process — we respect your time and communicate clearly at every step.

01

Application

Send your CV and a brief note about why you're interested. No cover letter templates needed.

02

Initial Call

30-minute call with our team to understand your background and answer your questions about the role.

03

Technical Assessment

A practical task or interview relevant to the role. We keep it focused and fair — no trick questions.

04

Offer

A clear, competitive offer with timeline and next steps. We move quickly once we've made a decision.
